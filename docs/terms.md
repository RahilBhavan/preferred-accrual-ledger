# Terms, scenarios and settlement limits

As of 2026-10-21. Base schedules are quarterly for STRF, STRE, STRK and STRD, and semi-monthly for STRC. Quarterly record dates are the 15th of March/June/September/December; nominal payments are quarter-end. STRC records on the prior month-end and 15th, with nominal payments on the 15th and month-end. Payments roll forward for the applicable business-day calendar. Rates are 10% for STRF/STRE/STRD, 8% for STRK and variable for STRC, on stated amounts of 100 in each currency. These are per-share terms, not guaranteed cash distributions.

Sources checked on primary sites:

- [2025 Form 10-K Exhibit 4.2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000105044626000020/ex42descriptionofregistere.htm) describes STRF, STRK and STRD terms. STRF unpaid dividends compound quarterly with an initial one percentage-point increase, further quarterly increases, and an 18% cap. STRK unpaid dividends compound at its regular rate. Undeclared STRD dividends do not accumulate. Declared but unpaid STRD is tracked separately from forfeiture.
- [STRE certificate](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000119312525280178/d205736dex31.htm) supplies euro terms and the step-up arrears rule. STRE has London/New York/T2 business-day requirements.
- [STRC amended certificate](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000119312526270366/d144149dex31.htm) supplies the existing semi-monthly schedule and compounding cadence.
- [October proxy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000119312526413159/d503578ddef14a.htm) proposes daily records for four USD series; STRE is excluded. The October 28 vote had not occurred at this cutoff. Post-cutoff note: Strategy's [8-K filed 2026-10-28](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000119312526435451/mstr-20261028.htm) reports, on preliminary voting results, that the requisite vote was obtained, subject to filing and effectiveness of the amended certificates (STRC daily from 2026-11-01; STRF, STRK, STRD from 2027-01-01). This does not change the frozen cache or outputs. `--scenario approved` assumes approval and effectiveness; the default `current` retains existing schedules.
- [October conditional declaration](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000119312526423400/mstr-20261015.htm) supplies the November daily table. All 30 amount/date rows reconcile in the explicit approval scenario.

The replay begins June 30, 2026 with sourced opening shares and zero opening arrears. It is not a reconstruction since issuance. The seed includes weekly share changes and declarations, but bank settlement is not supplied. The engine assumes declared amounts settle on their due dates; future undeclared obligations are assumed declared and paid, with the latest known STRC rate held constant unless a reset event is supplied. Outputs call these model settlements and mark them `status=assumed_settled`; `status=paid` appears only when a supplied payment event evidences the payment, and the CLI seed supplies none. Missing historical declarations are treated as unpaid in the engine, not evidence that Strategy actually missed a dividend.

Unpaid scenarios support current quarterly/semi-monthly terms and oldest-arrears-first allocation. Arrears use boundary-based compounding with pro-rata open periods; adjusted-date stubs and changing STRC rates within arrears periods are approximate. Daily-scenario missed payments are rejected rather than incorrectly reusing old compounding terms. STRK stock settlement is tracked as monetary value, not delivered share counts. The schedule's `payment_form` says `cash` for a STRK row covered by a cash dividend declaration (the [August 31, 2026 declaration](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000119312526377583/mstr-20260831.htm) names STRK among its "quarterly cash dividends") and `election_unknown` for undeclared STRK rows, because the company may elect Class A stock; conversion/floor adjustments are not modeled. Independent ticker simulations do not enforce cross-series legal dividend blockers, redemption, solvency, taxes or portfolio ownership transfers. Opening balances must be dated at replay start. EUR and USD remain separate.

Replay requires exactly one opening balance per included ticker at the start. Declarations become visible on their event date. `as_of` controls assumed future payments, not point-in-time availability of the supplied source set.
