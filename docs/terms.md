# Terms, scenarios and settlement limits

As of 2026-10-21. Base schedules are quarterly for STRF, STRE, STRK and STRD, and semi-monthly for STRC. Quarterly record dates are the 15th of March/June/September/December; nominal payments are quarter-end. STRC records on the prior month-end and 15th, with nominal payments on the 15th and month-end. Payments roll forward for the applicable business-day calendar. Rates are 10% for STRF/STRE/STRD, 8% for STRK and variable for STRC, on stated amounts of 100 in each currency. These are per-share terms, not guaranteed cash distributions.

Sources checked on primary sites:

- [2025 Form 10-K Exhibit 4.2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000105044626000020/ex42descriptionofregistere.htm) describes STRF, STRK and STRD terms. STRF unpaid dividends compound quarterly with an initial one percentage-point increase, further quarterly increases, and an 18% cap. STRK unpaid dividends compound at its regular rate. Undeclared STRD dividends do not accumulate. Declared but unpaid STRD is tracked separately from forfeiture.
- [STRE certificate](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000119312525280178/d205736dex31.htm) supplies euro terms and the step-up arrears rule. STRE has London/New York/T2 business-day requirements.
- [STRC amended certificate](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000119312526270366/d144149dex31.htm) supplies the existing semi-monthly schedule and compounding cadence.
- [October proxy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000119312526413159/d503578ddef14a.htm) proposes daily records for four USD series; STRE is excluded. The October 28 vote has not occurred at this cutoff. `--scenario approved` assumes approval and effectiveness; the default `current` retains existing schedules.
- [October conditional declaration](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000119312526423400/mstr-20261015.htm) supplies the November daily table. All 30 amount/date rows reconcile in the explicit approval scenario.

The replay begins June 30, 2026 with sourced opening shares and zero opening arrears. It is not a reconstruction since issuance. The seed includes weekly share changes and declarations, but bank settlement is not supplied. The engine assumes declared amounts settle on their due dates; future undeclared obligations are assumed declared and paid, with the latest known STRC rate held constant unless a reset event is supplied. Outputs call these model settlements. Missing historical declarations are treated as unpaid in the engine, not evidence that Strategy actually missed a dividend.

Unpaid scenarios support current quarterly/semi-monthly terms and oldest-arrears-first allocation. Arrears use boundary-based compounding with pro-rata open periods; adjusted-date stubs and changing STRC rates within arrears periods are approximate. Daily-scenario missed payments are rejected rather than incorrectly reusing old compounding terms. STRK stock settlement is tracked as monetary value, not delivered share counts; conversion/floor adjustments are not modeled. Independent ticker simulations do not enforce cross-series legal dividend blockers, redemption, solvency, taxes or portfolio ownership transfers. Opening balances must be dated at replay start. EUR and USD remain separate.
